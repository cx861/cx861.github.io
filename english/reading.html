第一章 阅读理解的题型

1.1 主旨大意题

考查内容

考查对文章整体内容的理解。

常见提问方式

  • What is the main idea of the passage?
  • What is the best title for this passage?
  • What does the passage mainly discuss?
  • The passage is primarily concerned with...

解题技巧

  • 关注文章首尾段
  • 注意每段的首句
  • 寻找文章中的高频词

定位方法：优先看首段提出的问题、末段给出的回答、各段首句共同重复的中心词。若选项只概括某一段，通常是以偏概全。

1.2 细节理解题

考查内容

考查对文章具体细节的理解。

常见提问方式

  • According to the passage, which of the following is true?
  • What can we learn from the passage?
  • The author mentions all of the following except...
  • Which of the following is mentioned as...

解题技巧

  • 根据关键词定位原文
  • 注意同义替换
  • 排除干扰选项

定位方法：题干中的人名、年份、专有名词、比较级、因果词最适合作定位。定位后不要只看一个词，要读完整句和前后一句。

1.3 推理判断题

考查内容

考查根据文章内容进行推理的能力。

常见提问方式

  • What can be inferred from the passage?
  • It can be concluded from the passage that...
  • The author suggests that...
  • Which of the following can be inferred?

解题技巧

  • 不要过度推断
  • 注意文中的暗示
  • 结合上下文理解

判断标准：正确推断必须能在原文找到依据，通常是原文换一种说法；需要常识脑补、扩大范围或加入新信息的选项慎选。

1.4 词义猜测题

考查内容

考查根据上下文猜测词义的能力。

常见提问方式

  • The word "..." in paragraph X probably means...
  • What does the word "..." refer to?
  • The phrase "..." is closest in meaning to...

解题技巧

  • 根据上下文推断
  • 注意近义词和反义词
  • 分析词根词缀

上下文线索：重点看解释说明、转折、并列、例证和指代。若考代词指代，答案通常在前一句或同句前半部分。

1.5 作者态度题

考查内容

考查对作者观点和态度的理解。

常见提问方式

  • What is the author's attitude towards...?
  • How does the author feel about...?
  • The tone of the passage is...

解题技巧

  • 注意作者使用的形容词和副词
  • 分析作者的论述方式
  • 区分客观描述和主观评价

态度词判断：正向常见 supportive/favorable/optimistic；负向常见 critical/skeptical/concerned；客观常见 objective/neutral。极端词如 indifferent/contemptuous 需有强证据。

1.6 例证题

考查内容

考查例子服务于哪个观点，而不是例子本身的细节。

常见提问方式

  • The author mentions ... to show that...
  • The example of ... is used to illustrate...
  • Why does the author refer to ...?

解题技巧

  • 例子前后的观点句优先，尤其是例子前一句。
  • 选项若只复述例子细节，通常不是答案。
  • 注意 for example, such as, case, illustrate 等信号词。

第二章 阅读理解的解题技巧

2.1 略读（Skimming）

目的

快速浏览文章，了解大意。

方法

  • 重点看标题、副标题
  • 阅读首段和末段
  • 阅读每段的首句

2.2 查读（Scanning）

目的

快速查找特定信息。

方法

  • 根据关键词定位
  • 关注数字、人名、地名

2.3 精读（Careful Reading）

目的

仔细阅读关键段落，理解作者意图。

方法

  • 分析句子结构
  • 理解长难句
  • 把握逻辑关系

2.4 排除法

技巧

  • 排除明显错误的选项
  • 比较选项之间的差异
  • 注意绝对化表述

小贴士：排除法是阅读理解中非常有效的解题方法，可以帮助你在不确定答案时提高正确率。

2.5 正确选项的同义替换规律

替换类型原文常见形式选项常见形式
词汇替换important, harmful, improvecrucial, damaging, enhance
词性转换decide, explain, reducedecision, explanation, reduction
上下义替换cars, buses, trainstransportation
正反改写not impossible / rarelypossible / not often
句式改写because A, BB results from A

正确答案通常不是原文照抄，而是“定位句 + 同义改写”。如果选项只和原文有个别简单词重合，但逻辑关系不一致，要警惕。

第三章 阅读理解的常见错误选项特征

3.1 偷换概念

选项中的概念与原文不符。

识别：把原文的对象、范围、时间或条件换掉，如“some”换成“all”，“may”换成“must”。

3.2 以偏概全

选项只涵盖文章部分内容。

识别：主旨题中最常见，选项看似正确但只对应某一段或某个例子。

3.3 无中生有

选项内容在原文中没有提及。

识别：选项符合常识但原文没有说，尤其是原因、目的、结果类表述。

3.4 绝对化表述

使用绝对化词汇（always, never, all, only等）。

3.5 过度推断

选项内容超出原文的合理推断范围。

识别：推断链条太长，或把作者的谨慎表述扩大成确定结论。

3.6 颠倒黑白

选项内容与原文意思相反。

识别：注意转折词、否定词和比较对象，很多错误项只改一个逻辑方向。

第四章 阅读理解的练习方法

4.1 真题练习

  • 历年真题是最好的练习材料
  • 按照考试时间限时练习
  • 分析错题原因

4.2 精读文章

  • 选择真题中的典型文章
  • 逐句翻译理解
  • 总结长难句结构

4.3 积累词汇

  • 整理真题中的高频词汇
  • 记忆近义词和反义词
  • 学习词根词缀

4.4 分析长难句

  • 识别句子主干
  • 分析修饰成分
  • 理解句子逻辑关系

注意：阅读理解需要长期积累和练习，建议每天坚持练习至少1-2篇。

第五章 阅读理解示例分析（非真题）

5.1 示例文章片段

The rise of artificial intelligence has led to significant changes in various industries.
Many companies are now using AI to improve efficiency and reduce costs.
However, there are concerns about the impact of AI on employment and privacy.

5.2 题目示例

Question: What is the main concern about AI mentioned in the passage?

A) Its high cost

B) Its impact on employment and privacy

C) Its limited applications

D) Its complexity

5.3 答案与解析

答案：B

解析：根据文章最后一句"there are concerns about the impact of AI on employment and privacy"，可以直接得出答案。

第六章 新题型专项

6.1 七选五

核心思路：先看空格前后句，再看选项中的指代词、逻辑词和复现词。

线索判断方法
指代关系this/that/these/such 必须能在上文找到明确指代对象
逻辑关系however/therefore/for example/in addition 决定前后句关系
词汇复现原词、同义词、上下义词反复出现，说明话题连续
句式衔接问答、总分、并列、转折等结构要前后匹配

6.2 排序题

核心思路：先确定首段和尾段，再找段落之间的衔接链。

  • 首段通常引出话题，不大量使用 this/these/they 等回指词。
  • 含有 however/therefore/in addition 的段落通常不是第一段。
  • 优先寻找“名词首次出现 → 代词回指”的顺序。
  • 若两个段落都能衔接，比较它们是否构成“观点 → 例子”或“问题 → 解决”的自然顺序。

6.3 小标题匹配

核心思路：小标题必须概括整段，而不是对应某个细节词。

  • 优先读段首句、段尾句和转折句。
  • 标题中的抽象词通常对应段内多个具体例子。
  • 若选项只覆盖段落一半内容，通常范围过窄。
  • 注意排除“关键词相同但中心不符”的选项。

6.4 新题型通用检查清单

  1. 先判断文章结构：总分、问题解决、时间顺序、观点反驳。
  2. 标出每段中心词和逻辑词。
  3. 利用代词回指确认前后句是否顺畅。
  4. 最后通读全文，检查话题是否连续、逻辑是否自然。

第七章 证据定位与干扰项拆解

自编材料：A survey of 200 students at one university found that those who planned weekly study sessions reported lower stress. The researchers noted that the survey did not establish whether planning itself reduced stress.

问题：Which conclusion is supported by the passage?

  1. Weekly planning eliminates stress for all students.
  2. Planning and lower reported stress were associated in this sample.
  3. The study proved that planning causes lower stress.
  4. Students without a plan cannot perform well academically.

答案 B：材料只支持“该样本内存在关联”。A 把 lower 变成 eliminates，并把一个学校的样本扩大到 all students；C 把相关变成因果，且与第二句相反；D 引入材料未谈的学业成绩与绝对否定。

复盘时给错项命名

  • 范围扩大：some → all，某项研究 → 所有情境。
  • 程度加重：may → must，降低 → 消除。
  • 对象偷换：作者立场与被引述者观点混淆。
  • 因果倒置或强加因果：同时出现不等于前者导致后者。
  • 答非所问：表述在文中成立，但没有回答题干限定的问题。

练习记录：题号 → 定位句 → 选项同义表达 → 被排除项的具体错误。含原词、绝对词或委婉词都不能单独决定对错，应比较整条命题。

主旨题：定位与验证

原创材料 1

A library once judged its success by the number of books it owned. Its shelves expanded, but many residents could not visit during working hours. After opening two evenings a week and offering advice by phone, the library reached people who had rarely used it. The director now measures success partly by whether residents can actually obtain help.

Question: Which best states the main idea?

  1. A large collection guarantees public value.
  2. Library value depends partly on usable access.
  3. Telephone advice should replace all books.
  4. Evening visitors are the only important users.
答案、证据与逐项排除

答案：B。

证据：The director now measures success partly by whether residents can actually obtain help.

  • A：把背景中的藏书数当成作者结论。
  • B：回应全段从数量到实际可及性的转变。
  • C：replace all 属无依据扩大。
  • D：only 把部分使用者变成唯一对象。

复查：用自己的话复述证据支持了什么，确认没有扩大范围或把相关改成因果。

原创材料 2

Planting more trees can make a neighbourhood attractive. Yet a planting campaign may achieve little if young trees die in their first summer. One town therefore moved part of its planting budget to watering and soil care. It planted fewer trees that year, but a larger proportion survived. The case highlights the importance of maintaining an investment after it is announced.

Question: The passage mainly argues that tree programmes should emphasize

  1. announcements over results.
  2. the largest possible annual planting target.
  3. care that helps planted trees survive.
  4. abandoning all new planting.
答案、证据与逐项排除

答案：C。

证据：The case highlights the importance of maintaining an investment after it is announced.

  • A：与结尾重结果相反。
  • B：把数量最大化当目标，忽略存活。
  • C：对应维护投入与实际存活。
  • D：从减少数量过度推成停止一切种植。

复查：用自己的话复述证据支持了什么，确认没有扩大范围或把相关改成因果。

原创材料 3

A new writing tool can suggest shorter sentences, but it cannot decide what an author wishes to claim. Students who accepted every suggestion sometimes removed necessary qualifications. Those who compared the suggestions with their intended meaning made more useful changes. The lesson is not to avoid the tool, but to use it while retaining responsibility for the argument.

Question: The best title is

  1. Using Writing Advice with Judgment
  2. Why Every Long Sentence Is Wrong
  3. A Tool That Understands Every Intention
  4. Ending the Use of Writing Software
答案、证据与逐项排除

答案：A。

证据：The lesson is not to avoid the tool, but to use it while retaining responsibility for the argument.

  • A：覆盖工具使用与作者判断两方面。
  • B：every 与文章保留限定的观点冲突。
  • C：与 cannot decide 相反。
  • D：与 not to avoid 相反。

复查：用自己的话复述证据支持了什么，确认没有扩大范围或把相关改成因果。

细节题：定位与验证

原创材料 1

The museum offers free entry on Friday evenings. Visitors must reserve a time online, and the offer covers the permanent collection only. A separate ticket is still needed for the temporary photography exhibition. Staff advise visitors without internet access to telephone the booking desk rather than arrive without a reservation.

Question: Which statement is correct?

  1. All exhibitions are free every Friday.
  2. A reservation is unnecessary for telephone users.
  3. Free visitors may enter at any time.
  4. The photography exhibition requires another ticket.
答案、证据与逐项排除

答案：D。

证据：A separate ticket is still needed for the temporary photography exhibition.

  • A：把 permanent collection 扩展到所有展览。
  • B：电话是预约渠道，不是免预约。
  • C：time reservation 排除任意时间。
  • D：与明确限定一致。

复查：用自己的话复述证据支持了什么，确认没有扩大范围或把相关改成因果。

原创材料 2

The repair service will collect faulty equipment within five working days after a request is approved. This period refers to collection, not completion of repairs. Customers must provide a description of the fault. Proof of purchase is required only when they request a repair under the warranty.

Question: Proof of purchase is required when customers

  1. report any problem.
  2. claim a warranty repair.
  3. ask for the collection date.
  4. receive a repaired item.
答案、证据与逐项排除

答案：B。

证据：Proof of purchase is required only when they request a repair under the warranty.

  • A：把 only when 的条件删掉。
  • B：保留 warranty 这一适用范围。
  • C：材料未设此条件。
  • D：将申请条件移到收货时点。

复查：用自己的话复述证据支持了什么，确认没有扩大范围或把相关改成因果。

原创材料 3

The college introduced a later bus for students attending evening classes. It runs on Tuesday and Thursday during teaching weeks. The existing daytime routes remain unchanged. A survey of evening students will be used to decide whether an additional day is needed, so the current timetable is a trial rather than a final arrangement.

Question: The later bus currently operates

  1. every day throughout the year.
  2. only during holidays.
  3. on two specified days in teaching weeks.
  4. instead of all daytime routes.
答案、证据与逐项排除

答案：C。

证据：It runs on Tuesday and Thursday during teaching weeks.

  • A：扩大频率与时间范围。
  • B：与 teaching weeks 相反。
  • C：准确保留两项时间限制。
  • D：与 remain unchanged 相反。

复查：用自己的话复述证据支持了什么，确认没有扩大范围或把相关改成因果。

推断题：定位与验证

原创材料 1

A workplace pilot allowed volunteers to choose their start times. They reported greater satisfaction at the end of the month. However, the volunteers already had more control over their work than employees who did not join. The researchers said a broader comparison would be needed before the result could be used to predict effects across the company.

Question: What can reasonably be inferred?

  1. The pilot alone does not establish a company-wide effect.
  2. Flexible hours harm all non-volunteers.
  3. Start times were the only difference between groups.
  4. Satisfaction proves that output increased.
答案、证据与逐项排除

答案：A。

证据：a broader comparison would be needed before the result could be used to predict effects across the company

  • A：从样本差异及推广限制推出证据边界。
  • B：材料未描述非参与者受损。
  • C：与 already had more control 冲突。
  • D：把满意度换成产出，并偷加因果。

复查：用自己的话复述证据支持了什么，确认没有扩大范围或把相关改成因果。

原创材料 2

An archive placed detailed descriptions of its photographs online. Most users downloaded the descriptions before requesting a high-resolution image. Requests became fewer, but staff noticed that they were more specific. The archive did not remove any photographs from its catalogue during this period.

Question: The new descriptions probably

  1. made the archive lose its photographs.
  2. eliminated every need for staff.
  3. prevented users from finding relevant material.
  4. helped users judge what to request.
答案、证据与逐项排除

答案：D。

证据：Requests became fewer, but staff noticed that they were more specific.

  • A：末句明确未删照片。
  • B：staff仍在处理请求。
  • C：更具体的请求不支持无法检索。
  • D：由预览信息及请求更具体合理推出。

复查：用自己的话复述证据支持了什么，确认没有扩大范围或把相关改成因果。

原创材料 3

Residents repeatedly missed a consultation held at midday. The organisers initially interpreted the low attendance as a lack of interest. When the same questions were discussed after working hours, many previously absent residents took part. Several explained that their jobs had prevented them from attending the earlier meetings.

Question: The later meeting suggests that

  1. all residents dislike consultations.
  2. practical access affected participation.
  3. the first meeting had already represented everyone.
  4. working residents never express opinions.
答案、证据与逐项排除

答案：B。

证据：Several explained that their jobs had prevented them from attending the earlier meetings.

  • A：与实际参与相反。
  • B：工作时间解释了参与障碍。
  • C：与原先缺席者未参与矛盾。
  • D：never 被后来的参与直接否定。

复查：用自己的话复述证据支持了什么，确认没有扩大范围或把相关改成因果。

词义题：定位与验证

原创材料 1

The report praised the new training course for its clear examples. It then qualified that praise by noting that the course had been tested with only a small group. The authors recommended a wider trial before any department treated the course as a complete replacement for existing training.

Question: Here qualified most nearly means

  1. gave a formal qualification to.
  2. repeated without limits.
  3. made less absolute.
  4. translated into another language.
答案、证据与逐项排除

答案：C。

证据：by noting that the course had been tested with only a small group

  • A：此处宾语是 praise，不是授予某人资格。
  • B：与随后限制范围相反。
  • C：用小样本限制赞扬的力度。
  • D：没有翻译语境。

复查：用自己的话复述证据支持了什么，确认没有扩大范围或把相关改成因果。

原创材料 2

The old model could predict demand in ordinary weeks. It failed to account for the sharp increase during festivals, when visitors arrived from outside the town. The revised model therefore included information on these temporary visitors instead of relying only on the number of permanent residents.

Question: Account for here means

  1. explain or allow for.
  2. open a bank account for.
  3. count all residents twice.
  4. take legal responsibility for.
答案、证据与逐项排除

答案：A。

证据：The revised model therefore included information on these temporary visitors

  • A：增加解释变量以解释原模型遗漏的需求。

阅读方法笔记 · 英语一阅读错因训练

阶段三：新题型衔接训练

共9组原创训练：句子填空、排序、小标题匹配各3组。其中一组为5空7选项；其余为局部衔接练习。先找指代对象与段落功能，再检验前后逻辑，不只比对重复词。

填句1：五空七选项

The library planned a repair workshop but did not know which items residents wanted to bring. [1] The replies showed that damaged bags and loose furniture parts were more common than broken electronic devices.

Not every request could be handled by the volunteers. [2] This limit was stated on the booking page so that visitors would not arrive expecting a service the team could not provide.

Before the first event, the volunteers met for a practice session. [3] This rehearsal helped them notice which instructions were unclear to beginners.

The first workshop attracted mainly retired residents. Several people with daytime jobs later asked for another opportunity. [4] The later event brought in a different group without replacing the original session.

The organisers counted visits, but they also recorded whether each visitor left with an item repaired or a clear next step. [5] They wanted the measure of success to reflect useful outcomes rather than attendance alone.

  1. To make attendance possible for these residents, the library added an evening session.
  2. Requests involving electronic devices were directed to professional repair services.
  3. For that reason, the report distinguished completed repairs from visits that provided advice only.
  4. It first asked residents to describe their items in a short survey.
  5. All visitors were required to demonstrate expert repair skills before booking.
  6. They practised explaining a simple repair to a person unfamiliar with the tools.
  7. The team decided that the number of visitors was the only result worth recording.
顺序与衔接证据

答案：1—D，2—B，3—F，4—A，5—C。

  1. D的调查引出The replies；回复不可能先于调查。
  2. B交代可处理项目的边界，This limit回指该限制。
  3. F具体说明练习解释修理步骤，This rehearsal回指练习。
  4. A回应工作时间障碍，The later event回指新增晚场。
  5. C说明两种有用结果的记录方式，与末句outcomes而非人数一致。

E凭空设置专家门槛，与beginner语境不合；G的only与末段多种指标冲突。每句都要同时接住上文和下文。

填句2：局部前后照应

An archive put basic records online. Some users had slow connections, so the designers avoided large images on the first screen. [1] Detailed files remained available through a separate link.

  1. The archive deleted every detailed file.
  2. A lightweight page allowed more users to reach the descriptions.
  3. Only professional researchers could open the first screen.
  4. The designers stopped asking who could use the service.
答案与排除

答案：B。B解释轻量首页为何回应slow connections，且与详细文件另设链接兼容。A与remained冲突；C新增用户限制；D否定了改版对访问条件的回应。

填句3：局部前后照应

The class compared two summaries of the same article. One kept the author’s qualifications; the other replaced may with will. [1] The exercise showed why a shorter summary is not necessarily a more accurate one.

  1. Both summaries therefore had exactly the same meaning.
  2. The original article contained no qualifications.
  3. No summary can be accurate.
  4. The second version made the claim more certain than the original.
答案与排除

答案：D。D承接may→will造成的语气增强，并支撑末句准确性。A忽视差异，B与原文有qualifications冲突，C由个例扩展成全称否定。

排序1：五段全用一次

  1. The labels were then tested with a small group of first-time visitors.
  2. A museum noticed that visitors repeatedly asked where the temporary exhibition began.
  3. This feedback led the team to shorten two labels and enlarge the arrows.
  4. To address the problem, staff designed a set of new directional labels.
  5. Several members of that group found the wording clear but the arrows too small.
顺序与唯一衔接链

答案：B→D→A→E→C。

B提出找路问题→D设计指引→A测试指引→E报告测试反馈→C依据反馈修改。that group指首次参观者，This feedback指文字清楚但箭头小。

复查动作：把每个this/these/that的指代写出来，再检查“问题—处理—反馈—结果”的时间顺序。

排序2：五段全用一次

  1. As a result, the next meeting began with decisions rather than a search for missing information.
  2. The team first listed the questions that the report had left unanswered.
  3. Before that meeting, a named member checked each item and attached the missing evidence.
  4. It then assigned an owner and a deadline to each question.
  5. These assignments were entered in a shared document for the following meeting.
顺序与唯一衔接链

答案：B→D→E→C→A。

B先识别问题→D分配负责人和期限→E共享记录→C下次会议前核验→A描述效果。that meeting指following meeting，不能把结果放在准备步骤之前。

复查动作：把每个this/these/that的指代写出来，再检查“问题—处理—反馈—结果”的时间顺序。

排序3：五段全用一次

  1. Only after this check did the group write its final recommendation.
  2. Students were asked to recommend a location for a study room.
  3. They compared these alternatives using the same questions about access and noise.
  4. They began by identifying three possible sites on campus.
  5. The comparison revealed that the quietest site was difficult to reach, so they checked whether quietness alone should determine the choice.
顺序与唯一衔接链

答案：B→D→C→E→A。

B设定任务→D列三地点→C同尺度比较→E发现安静与可达性的权衡→A复核后推荐。these alternatives必须有先行地点，this check回指重新审视。

复查动作：把每个this/these/that的指代写出来，再检查“问题—处理—反馈—结果”的时间顺序。

小标题匹配1：三段四选项

Paragraph 1. A feedback note should identify the exact point where the reader became confused. “Unclear” is less useful than a question about who performed an action in the second sentence.

Paragraph 2. The writer should revise one passage and ask a reader to explain its meaning again. A correction is useful only if it resolves the original difficulty.

Paragraph 3. Several comments may refer to the same underlying problem. Grouping them before revision can prevent the writer from changing the same paragraph repeatedly.

  1. Test the revision
  2. Locate the difficulty
  3. Group related comments
  4. Replace readers with automatic scores
答案与段落功能

按段落顺序：B、A、C。

第一段定位问题；第二段复核修改效果；第三段合并同源反馈。D没有得到任何段落支持，不能因出现feedback就凭印象选工具。

小标题匹配2：三段四选项

Paragraph 1. Before opening a service to everyone, identify which tasks it is designed to support and which requests fall outside its scope.

Paragraph 2. A sign-up form may be simple for regular users but confusing for newcomers. Watch a new visitor attempt it before concluding that it is easy.

Paragraph 3. The opening month may include temporary help from volunteers. Record that support so that later costs are not compared with an unrealistic starting point.

  1. Count only the first month
  2. Observe a newcomer
  3. Keep a record of temporary support
  4. Define the service boundary
答案与段落功能

按段落顺序：D、B、C。

第一段界定范围；第二段观察实际用户；第三段记录临时支持。A与第三段要求的可比成本相反。

小标题匹配3：三段四选项

Paragraph 1. An article may quote a dramatic claim to examine it rather than to endorse it. Mark who made the claim and find the author’s response.

Paragraph 2. A statement about some volunteers should not become a conclusion about all workers. Preserve the population and the qualifiers.

Paragraph 3. After choosing an option, explain how the evidence supports each part of it. A repeated keyword alone does not establish the same meaning.

  1. Track the speaker
  2. Verify the whole option
  3. Preserve the scope
  4. Choose the longest sentence
答案与段落功能

按段落顺序：A、C、B。

分别对应态度归属、范围限定、逐项核证。D是与证据无关的猜题规则。
