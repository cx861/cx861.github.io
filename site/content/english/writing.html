第一章 写作总论

一、考情分析

项目英语(一)英语(二)
小作文(A节)约100词应用文，10分同
大作文(B节)160-200词短文，20分约150词，15分
图画作文概率40%20%
图表作文概率40%70%
材料作文概率20%10%
应用文-书信概率70%同
应用文-通知告示概率20%同
应用文-纪要概率10%同

⭐ 英语(一)重点：图画作文+材料作文+应用文。英语(二)重点：图表作文+应用文。无论哪种，都应全面复习。

二、评分标准（第五档为最高）

档位小作文大作文(一)标准
第五档9-10分17-20分很好地完成任务，语言丰富准确，层次清晰
第四档7-8分13-16分较好地完成任务，有个别错误
第三档5-6分9-12分基本完成任务，有一些错误
第二档3-4分5-8分未能恰当完成任务，错误较多
第一档1-2分1-4分未完成任务，错误很多

评分四大重点：

  1. 内容要点覆盖是否完整
  2. 文章组织和连贯性
  3. 语法结构和词汇的多样性及准确性
  4. 文体格式和语体的恰当性

⭐ 书写是第一印象！ 阅卷教师每天批阅数千份试卷，书写差的直接影响得分。建议练习手写印刷体或衡水体。

三、复习方法

四大背诵法：

  1. 搞清逻辑脉络：理清范文的行文结构
  2. 背熟中文译文：先理解中文意思
  3. 多听英文录音：跟读模仿，练听说
  4. 中英互译：将英文译成中文，再译回英文对照

背诵标准：大作文15分钟内默写完，小作文8分钟内默写完，没有错误。

三阶段法：背诵 → 默写（每晚1篇，红笔改正） → 仿写（用背过的范文写类似题目）

第二章 应用文写作（小作文）

一、常考类型

类型考频核心要点
建议信★★★★★说明目的→2-3条建议+理由→期望采纳
邀请信★★★★说明邀请→活动详情(时间/地点/内容)→期待参加
推荐信★★★★推荐对象→2-3条推荐理由→总结推荐
投诉信★★★表明投诉→2-3个具体问题+影响→要求解决
申请信★★★申请目的→自身条件/优势→期望回复
道歉信★★表明歉意→解释原因→补救措施
感谢信★★表明感谢→具体感谢内容→再次致谢
辞职信★表明辞职→原因→感谢+交接安排
通知/告示★★★★标题(NOTICE)→活动目的+时间地点+要求→落款
纪要★会议基本信息→主要内容→决议事项

二、万能框架

书信格式：

Dear [称呼],

第一段(1-2句): 表明写信目的

I am writing to [express/invite/suggest/complain/apply...]

第二段(3-4句): 具体内容

To begin with, ... Furthermore, ... In addition, ...

第三段(1-2句): 礼貌收尾

I would appreciate it if you could... / I am looking forward to...

Yours sincerely,

Li Ming

通知格式：

NOTICE
[日期, 如 May 10, 2026]

[正文: 目的+活动详情+要求/注意事项]

[发布单位, 如 The Student Union]

三、高分句型速查

开头句：

  • I am writing to express my sincere gratitude for...
  • I am writing to make a complaint about...
  • I am writing to apply for the position of...
  • I am writing to extend my warm invitation to...

过渡句：

  • The reasons are as follows.
  • My suggestions are listed below.
  • I would like to bring to your attention that...

结尾句：

  • I would greatly appreciate it if you could take my suggestions into consideration.
  • Thank you for your time and consideration.
  • I am looking forward to your favorable reply.
  • Please accept my sincere apologies.

第四章 英语(一)大作文强化

核心定位：英语(一)大作文重点准备图画作文，同时兼顾材料作文。关键是读懂寓意、展开论证、写出清晰三段结构。

一、英语一大作文审题流程

步骤操作目标
1看图画主体确定人物/物体/场景，避免描述跑偏
2看动作与对比抓住冲突、反差、因果或象征关系
3看文字说明标题、标语、人物语言常直接提示主题
4提炼寓意从表层画面上升到品质、社会现象或价值观
5确定立场赞成、批评、呼吁或提出建议

二、寓意提炼模板

画面特征可提炼主题常用表达
人物互助、协作完成任务合作、责任、互助cooperation, mutual support, social responsibility
困难前坚持行动坚持、行动、乐观perseverance, taking action, optimism
沉迷手机、忽视现实交流科技影响、人际关系technology addiction, face-to-face communication
传统文化与现代生活并置文化传承、文化自信cultural heritage, cultural confidence
环境破坏或资源浪费环保、可持续发展environmental protection, sustainable development

三、第二段展开角度

  • 个人层面：影响个人成长、学习效率、价值观和行为习惯。
  • 社会层面：影响公共秩序、社会风气、人际关系和群体利益。
  • 文化层面：涉及传统文化、价值传承、文化交流和民族认同。
  • 时代层面：联系科技发展、信息时代、竞争压力和生活方式变化。

四、英语一材料作文处理

若题目不给图画而给文字材料，写法仍可使用三段式：

段落写法
第一段概括材料核心，不逐句翻译原文
第二段解释材料反映的现象或道理，展开两到三个原因/意义
第三段给出个人观点、建议或呼吁

五、英语一大作文易错点

  • 第一段不要只写“有一幅图”，必须写清图中人物动作和核心反差。
  • 不要把寓意写得过窄，例如只停留在“一个人在看手机”，应上升到“technology addiction”。
  • 第二段不要堆模板句，要至少有两个具体论证角度。
  • 结尾不要突然换主题，建议回扣第二段中心词。
  • 少用生僻词，优先保证语法正确、结构清楚、表达自然。

附录：复习建议

阶段时间任务
基础期9月30日前背熟20篇范文(大小作文各10篇)
巩固期10-11月每天默写1-2篇，循环复习
冲刺期12月仿写预测题目，控制时间模拟练习

⭐ 王江涛核心建议：最笨的方法往往是最好的方法——背熟20篇范文，反复默写，再去考场上灵活套用。功夫不负有心人！

第四章 应用文十大必背真题范文

范文001：通知/告示——2010年英语(一)

Directions: You are supposed to write for the Postgraduates' Association a notice to recruit volunteers for an international conference on globalization.

经典范文：

NOTICE

January 9, 2010

Volunteers are needed for the International Conference on Globalization to be held on this campus on February 28, 2010. The specific requirements and responsibilities are listed below.

To begin with, applicants should have a strong sense of responsibility and team spirit. Furthermore, it is preferable that they have relevant voluntary experience. In addition, volunteers are expected to provide interpretation services and reception work during the conference.

Those who are interested in this activity may sign up with the monitor of their classes before February 14, 2010. Everyone is welcome to join it.

Postgraduates' Association

参考译文：2010年1月9日。国际全球化会议将于2010年2月28日在本校举行，现招募志愿者。具体要求与职责如下：首先，申请者须有强烈的责任心和团队精神。其次，最好有相关志愿经验。此外，志愿者需在会议期间提供口译和接待服务。对此活动感兴趣的同学请于2010年2月14日前在本班班长处报名。欢迎大家参加。

精彩词汇：requirement n. 要求，必要条件 | volunteer n. 志愿者 | reciprocal adj. 互惠的 | conference n. 会议

范文002：感谢信——2010年英语(二)

Directions: You have just come back from the U.S. as a member of a Sino-American cultural exchange program. Write a letter to your American colleague to express your thanks for his/her warm reception.

经典范文：

Dear Bob,

I have just returned to China and I would like to express my sincere gratitude for your warm reception and hospitality during my stay in the U.S.

It was very kind of you to take time out of your busy schedule to accompany me around New York. I was deeply impressed by the places of interest we visited, including the Statue of Liberty and the Metropolitan Museum. Your vivid introduction to American culture has broadened my horizons.

I do hope that you will pay a visit to China someday so that I could have the opportunity to repay your kindness. Please give my best regards to your family.

Yours sincerely,

Li Ming

参考译文：我刚回到中国，想对我留美期间你的热情款待表示衷心感谢。非常感谢你在百忙中抽出时间陪我游览纽约。我们参观的名胜包括自由女神像和大都会博物馆给我留下了深刻印象，你对美国文化的生动介绍拓宽了我的视野。希望你有朝一日能访问中国，让我有机会回报你的好意。

精彩词汇：reception n. 接待，欢迎 | gratitude n. 感谢 | hospitality n. 好客，热情 | broaden one's horizons 拓宽视野

范文003：建议信——2012年英语(一)

Directions: Some international students are coming to your university. Write them an email to extend your welcome and provide some suggestions for their campus life here.

经典范文：

Dear friends,

On behalf of the Students' Union of Beijing Foreign Studies University, I would like to extend our heartfelt welcome to all of you.

To help you adapt to the campus life here, I would like to offer several practical suggestions. First, it is advisable to participate in various extracurricular activities organized by the Students' Union to make new friends. Second, the university library with abundant resources is well worth visiting. Last but not least, you may consult the international student office if you encounter any difficulties.

I genuinely hope that you will enjoy your stay here and find the above proposals beneficial. Should you have any further questions, please feel free to contact me.

Yours sincerely,

Li Ming

参考译文：亲爱的朋友们，我谨代表北外学生会向你们表示衷心欢迎。为帮助你们适应校园生活，我提供几条实用建议。首先，建议参加学生会组织的各类课外活动结交新朋友。第二，资源丰富的大学图书馆很值得一去。最后，如遇困难可以咨询留学生办公室。真心希望你们在此过得愉快，以上建议能有所帮助。

精彩词汇：extend v. 提供，给予 | heartfelt adj. 衷心的 | practical adj. 实用的 | advisable adj. 明智的，可取的 | extracurricular adj. 课外的

范文004：投诉信——2012年英语(二)

Directions: Suppose you have found something wrong with the electronic dictionary that you bought from an online store the other day. Write an email to the customer service center to make a complaint and demand a prompt solution.

经典范文：

Dear Sir or Madam,

I bought an electronic dictionary from your online store the other day, but I found something wrong with it and therefore I am writing to make a complaint.

The dictionary cannot be properly recharged, and its screen frequently freezes, which has greatly inconvenienced my daily use. To resolve the problem, I would like you to either replace it with a new one or give me a full refund.

I would greatly appreciate it if you could address this issue at your earliest convenience. I am looking forward to your prompt reply.

Yours sincerely,

Li Ming

参考译文：尊敬的先生/女士：我前几天在贵网店购买了一部电子词典，但发现有问题，因此写信投诉。该词典无法正常充电，屏幕频繁卡顿，严重影响了我的日常使用。为解决此问题，希望贵方予以更换或全额退款。如能尽快处理，将不胜感激。

精彩词汇：recharge v. 给电池充电 | refund n./v. 退款 | inconvenience v. 给…带来不便 | address v. 处理，解决

范文005：邀请信——2013年英语(一)

Directions: Write an email to invite a foreign professor to be a judge for the upcoming English speech contest.

经典范文：

Dear Professor Smith,

On behalf of the "Talents" English Speech Contest Committee, I am greatly honored to invite you to serve as a judge for the competition to be held in the auditorium on December 20, 2013.

As one of the leading experts in the field of English language teaching, your participation will undoubtedly make the contest a more prestigious event. The contest will start at 7:00 p.m. and last approximately two hours. Contestants will deliver a three-minute prepared speech followed by a two-minute impromptu speech and a Q&A session.

We would be more than delighted if you could accept this invitation. I am looking forward to your favorable reply at your earliest convenience.

Yours sincerely,

Li Ming

参考译文：亲爱的史密斯教授，我谨代表"英才"英语演讲比赛组委会，荣幸地邀请您担任将于2013年12月20日在大礼堂举行的比赛的评委。作为英语教学领域的顶尖专家，您的参与将毫无疑问使比赛更负盛名。比赛晚7点开始，约2小时。选手先做3分钟备稿演讲，再进行2分钟即兴演讲和问答环节。如蒙应允，不胜欣喜。期待尽快收到您的肯定答复。

精彩词汇：talent n. 人才，天赋 | prestigious adj. 有声望的 | impromptu adj. 即兴的 | auditorium n. 礼堂

范文006：推荐信——2017年英语(一)

Directions: You are to write an email to James Cook, a newly arrived Australian professor, recommending some tourist attractions in your city.

经典范文：

Dear Professor Cook,

We are greatly honored to welcome you to our university. Now I would like to recommend some tourist attractions in Beijing for your reference.

Given that you have shown a keen interest in traditional Chinese culture, the Palace Museum would be the primary choice. It is also known as the Forbidden City, where you can appreciate the magnificent ancient Chinese architecture. Next, the Great Wall, one of the Seven Wonders of the World, is well worth visiting. Hiking on the Great Wall will be an unforgettable experience.

I hope my suggestions will be of some help and that you will enjoy your stay in Beijing.

Yours sincerely,

Li Ming

参考译文：库克教授，很荣幸欢迎您来我校。现在我想为您推荐北京的一些旅游景点供参考。鉴于您对中国传统文化兴趣浓厚，故宫当为首选。它又名紫禁城，您可在此欣赏宏伟的中国古代建筑。接下来，世界七大奇迹之一的长城非常值得一游，在长城上徒步将是一次难忘的体验。希望我的建议有所帮助，祝您在北京过得愉快。

精彩词汇：primary adj. 主要的 | magnificent adj. 宏伟的 | architecture n. 建筑 | unforgettable adj. 难忘的

范文007：道歉信——2018年英语(二)

Directions: Suppose you have to cancel your travel plan and cannot visit Professor Smith as you promised. Write him an email to apologize and explain the situation.

经典范文：

Dear Professor Smith,

I am looking forward to meeting you as planned during my trip to New York. Unfortunately, I am writing to tell you that I have to cancel the visit.

The reason is that I have just been informed that I am required to attend an academic conference in Boston, which coincides exactly with my scheduled visit to you. I deeply regret that I cannot keep my promise. I was wondering whether I could pay you a visit next month when I am free.

Please accept my sincere apology and I hope you can understand my situation. I am looking forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,

Li Ming

参考译文：史密斯教授，我本期待着按计划在纽约之行中与您见面。不幸的是，我不得不写信告诉您我须取消此次访问。原因是我刚被告知须参加波士顿的一个学术会议，恰好与我计划探望您的时间冲突。我深感遗憾不能守约。不知下月有空时能否再去探望您？请接受我诚挚的歉意，望您谅解。

精彩词汇：unfortunately adv. 不幸地 | coincide v. 同时发生，巧合 | regret v. 遗憾，懊悔 | apology n. 道歉

范文008：建议+介绍——2023年真题(一)

Directions: Read the following email from an international student and write a reply.

经典范文：

Dear Paul,

I was really excited to hear that you are going to make an oral report about an ancient Chinese scientist. In regard to this, Bi Sheng would be an ideal candidate.

In the mid-11th century during the Song Dynasty, Bi Sheng invented the movable-type printing, which was a revolutionary breakthrough in the history of printing. Prior to his invention, books had to be copied by hand or printed from wood blocks, both of which were extremely time-consuming and labor-intensive. His invention greatly enhanced the efficiency of printing and facilitated the spread of knowledge and culture.

I hope the information above will be of assistance to your report. Should you need any further help, please do not hesitate to contact me.

Yours sincerely,

Li Ming

参考译文：亲爱的保罗，听说你要做关于一位中国古代科学家的口头报告，真让人兴奋。就此而言，毕昇是理想人选。11世纪中叶宋代，毕昇发明了活字印刷术，这是印刷史上革命性的突破。在此之前，书籍必须手工抄写或雕版印刷，极为耗时费力。他的发明大大提高了印刷效率，促进了知识和文化的传播。希望以上信息对你有帮助。

开头句型

There is no denying that...不可否认…
Recently, the issue of...has aroused widespread concern.近来…问题引起了广泛关注。

句型中文

Nothing is more important than...没有什么比…更重要。
It is universally acknowledged that...众所周知…

结尾句型

句型中文
From what has been discussed above, we may safely draw the conclusion that...综上所述，我们可以肯定地得出结论…

附录二：写作复习时间表

阶段时间任务标准
基础期9月30日前背熟20篇范文(大小作文各10篇)大作文15分钟默写、小作文8分钟默写无误
巩固期10-11月每天默写1-2篇，循环复习红笔改正拼写/标点/语法错误
冲刺期12月仿写预测题目，控制时间模拟大小作文共45-50分钟完成
日常每天练字10分钟(手写印刷体/衡水体)书写工整清晰

⭐ 王江涛寄语：最笨的方法往往是最好的方法。背熟20篇范文，反复默写，考场灵活套用。你付出的每一滴汗水都不会白流！
