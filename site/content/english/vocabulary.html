Part 4 阅读/写作同义替换专题

用途：阅读中用于识别正确选项的同义改写，写作中用于替换基础词，避免表达单一。

阅读定位高频同义替换

基础表达常见替换适用场景
importantcrucial, vital, essential, significant主旨题、态度题
causelead to, result in, give rise to, trigger因果定位
because ofdue to, owing to, thanks to, as a result of原因分析
changeshift, transform, alter, modify趋势/观点变化
increaserise, grow, climb, expand图表、阅读数据
decreasedecline, drop, fall, shrink图表、阅读数据
problemissue, challenge, difficulty, obstacle社会问题类文章
showindicate, reveal, suggest, demonstrate例证题、图表作文
helpcontribute to, facilitate, promote, enable积极影响
harmdamage, undermine, threaten, impair消极影响

写作升级替换

低分表达升级表达
more and more peoplean increasing number of people
very importantof great importance / play a vital role
good forbe beneficial to / exert a positive influence on
bad forbe harmful to / have a negative impact on
thinkhold the view that / maintain that
many reasonsseveral factors account for this phenomenon
solve the problemaddress/tackle the problem
try to domake efforts to do / endeavor to do

同义替换使用原则

  • 阅读中不要只看单词相同，重点看逻辑关系是否一致。
  • 写作中优先替换高频基础词，不要为了高级而牺牲准确性。
  • 动词替换要注意搭配，例如 solve 可换成 address/tackle，但不能随意换成 deal 而漏掉 with。
  • 形容词替换要注意语气强弱，例如 possible 和 inevitable 不能互换。
